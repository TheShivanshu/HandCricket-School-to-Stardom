School to Stardom

Two fists. One number each. Read your rival, and take it from the classroom to the National Cup.

Levels

Level 0

Bench partner

Your bench partner Mohit. One wicket, 12 balls an innings, best of three. Win the series to unlock Level 1.

Level 1

Class Championship

Four classmates, then the Class Champion. Each has habits and tells. Win at least 3 of the first 4 to earn the title match.

Level 2

Inter-Section Championship

Five a side for 10-A against 10-B, 10-C and 10-D. Earn your place in trials, then keep it match by match.

Level 3

Inter-Class Championship

Eight a side, ten overs, against Classes 9, 11 and 12. The selectors pick the Class 10 squad from how you played in Level 2.

Level 4

Inter-School Championship

The School World Cup. Eleven a side, fifteen overs, eight schools in two groups, then semi-finals and the final, each on a different ground.

Level 5

District Championship

The best school teams in the district. Twelve teams, a District XI picked purely on form, and a different town for every match.

Level 6

State Championship

Sixteen teams from across the state, elite captains who scout you, and a State XI picked purely on form. Four group winners reach the semi-finals.

Level 7

National Hand Cricket Cup

Delhi XI against the best sixteen state teams in the country. A qualifier, groups, quarter-finals, a semi-final and the grand final.

  • Show a number from 1 to 6 together. Same number: the batter is out. Otherwise the batter scores his own number.
  • Odd or even for the toss. Watch his last balls and listen to what he says: everyone has habits.
  • Score 50 in one innings and the whole class stands up for you. 100, 150 and 200 get bigger.
